## Cross-references

None. `INC-201` is the far end of every chain: the other artifacts point *at* it.

The phrase **`Incident X`** is the only thing that makes `--around "incident X"`
resolvable, and it must appear here, in the earliest-dated document of the story.
Event resolution searches the index for the event text, takes the **earliest**
document among the top hits, and centers a `± query.event_window` window on that
document's `created_at`. Keep the phrase out of anything dated earlier than this
ticket or the window shifts.
